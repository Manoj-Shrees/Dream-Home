# Dream Home — Interactive 3D Architectural Villa & MEP Studio

**Live Site:** https://manoj-shrees.github.io/Dream-Home/

An interactive 3D architectural showcase, real-home eye-level walkthrough, and full MEP (Water, Ventilation, Drainage & LPG Gas) simulation for a 16-Aana luxury modern villa (G + 2 + Rooftop, ~4,000 sq ft) built with Three.js and modern responsive web standards.

## Key Features
- **Real-Home First-Person Walkthrough & 8-Stop Guided Tour:** Walk inside at human eye level (`WASD` / Arrow Keys or touch D-pad) with quick room jumps, floor step controls, walking head-bob, and proximity auto-opening doors.
- **Complete 3D MEP & Straining Showcase (X-Ray & Spotlight Modes):** Color-coded animated piping & ductwork for **18,000 L Cold Water Supply** (15,000 L UG UV/RO vault + 3,000 L roof tanks), **500 L Solar Hot Water**, **Sanitary DWV Soil Stacks → Bio-Septic Chamber**, **Rainwater Harvesting + Multi-Mesh Vortex Pre-Filter**, **Stormwater Draining & Strainer Lines** (front gate/garage/perimeter stainless trench grates, 4 corner silt catch-basin strainers, baffled kitchen/BBQ grease trap, 4 inline brass Y-strainers, rooftop/balcony dome leaf strainers & front-right non-return outfall flap valve), and **HVAC/Exhaust Ventilation** (rooftop ERV condenser, spinning rotary roof turbo-vent, stainless kitchen & BBQ chimney hoods, and spinning exhaust fans in all 8 bathrooms).
- **Waterproof Lockable Dual-LPG Gas Vault & All-Kitchen Piping:** External elevated 24 cm concrete rain plinth, perforated drain grate, sloped monsoon hood, louvered rain baffles, keyed brass padlock + deadbolt security door (`🔒 Gas Lock`), twin cylinders with auto-changeover regulator, and safety-yellow copper gas piping with drip-loops & isolation valves to both Ground Floor Main Kitchen and Rooftop BBQ Chef Kitchen.
- **Luxury Spa Bathrooms & Toilets (All 8 Baths/WCs):** Freestanding soaking tub with teak candle caddy, concealed-cistern porcelain toilets with chrome dual-flush plates, bidet sprays & tissue holders, vessel vanities, backlit halo mirrors, frameless rain showers with recessed niches, heated towel racks, and spinning exhaust fans.
- **Wall/Ceiling Decor & Live Animated TVs/Gaming:** Procedural framed gallery art, fluted marble/walnut feature walls, velvet/sheer curtains, dropped LED cove ceilings with spinning ceiling fans, and real-time animated screens (4K HDR living/family/master TVs, 240 FPS triple-monitor cyber-racing PC rig, live esports console arena TV, rotating RGB PC fans, karaoke equalizer TV, and live BIM/CAD office monitors).
- **5-Stage Live Water Filtration Plant & Simulation:** Animated purification vault (Vortex Silt → Sand FRP → Activated Carbon → Hard-Water Softener → UV-C 254 nm & RO Membrane) with real-time color-shifting water flow droplets, digital TDS SCADA telemetry (`340 ppm → 28 ppm`), and under-sink RO tap.
- **VR Motion Sim-Racing Car Rig & Arcade Studio:** Hydraulic motion-rig VR racing cockpit with actively turning force-feedback steering wheel, 3-pedal box, shifter & dedicated sim screen, retro neon arcade cabinet, hexagonal Nanoleaf RGB panels, streaming mic/ring light, triple curved 240 FPS PC monitors & esports console bay.
- **Organic Kitchen Vegetable Farm & Stair-Top Observatory Dome:** Sunlit rear-left vegetable garden with 3 raised cedar beds (tomatoes/chilies on bamboo trellises, cabbages/greens, carrots/herbs), glass seed-starter cold-frame greenhouse, rainwater drip barrel & composter; plus a champagne-gold radial glass skylight observatory dome, Party Shed sky-bridge, and copper lightning rod crowning the spiral staircase.
- **Zero-Glitch Wall-Aligned Concealed Piping:** Strictly orthogonal 90° wall-aligned plumbing, drainage, ventilation, and gas chases — 100% invisible to the naked eye in Standard view and revealed cleanly in MEP X-Ray / Spotlight modes.
- **12 Exterior & 8 Interior Luxury Color Combinations:** Switch live across 12 architectural facade/trim/mullion palettes (Ivory & Gold, Midnight Navy, Chitwan Terracotta, Obsidian & Rose Gold, Emerald Slate, Arctic Chrome, Espresso Copper, Royal Plum, etc.) and 8 interior upholstery/drapery palettes.
- **Rolls-Royce Phantom & Mercedes-Benz S-Class AMG 2-Car Garage:** Custom 3D models featuring the two-tone Rolls-Royce (Parthenon chrome grille, golden Spirit of Ecstasy & coachline) beside the metallic silver Mercedes-Benz AMG (3-pointed star grille, Multibeam LEDs & red brake calipers) behind the motorized 12-slat rolling garage door.
- **62-Step Spiral Staircase, Open Floor Doorways & Armored Safety Doors:** Individual marble & walnut helical steps with gold nosing and LED glow connecting seamlessly into open floor doorways across every level, fitted with interactive hinged biometric safety doors (`🛡️ Safety Doors`), full perimeter & rooftop metal security barriers, executive family office chairs, and motorized unfolding curtains (`🪟 Curtains`) across all panoramic glazing.
- **Zero-Shift Responsive Studio Controls:** Fixed-weight ARIA-driven toolbars (`role="toolbar" focusgroup="toolbar wrap"`), non-shifting toggle LED dots, 17 interactive 3D room/MEP pins, and dual-engine fullscreen with live Model Controls.

## Run Locally
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Then open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
