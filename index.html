Spiral Atrium Back Side

What is behind the Glass Spiral Staircase?

The rear side of the spiral staircase is the villa's vertical circulation bridge & concealed MEP core—linking all 4 levels seamlessly without cluttering the front glass façade:

  • Floor Landing Bridges & Doorways: Curved white landings with glass balustrades bridge the spiral stair into both the left main wing (Living/Lounges/Master) and right pavilion (Guest WC, Bedrooms 1/4/6, Study & Rooftop Laundry).
  • Backlit Walnut Feature Spine: Fluted timber & travertine architectural backing with warm cove LEDs frames the chandelier from the front.
  • Concealed MEP Chase / Lift Shaft: Houses cold/hot water risers, rainwater downpipes, electrical ducts, and optional future compact home-lift space.

Foundation & Multi-Hazard Safety

Earthquake, Monsoon Flood & Thunderstorm Defense

Click Foundation & Safety or toggle 🌊 Flood Gate to inspect the full subterranean structure and disaster-resilience engineering:

  • Deep Pile-Raft Foundation: 16 bored cast-in-situ RCC friction piles (Ø 0.56 m, -3.85 m) tied into a crystalline waterproof M30 mat slab, plinth tie-beams & HDPE damp-proof membrane.
  • Earthquake Safety (NBC-105 Zone V): 16 laminated steel & lead-rubber Seismic Base Isolators, full-height ductile shear-wall core with X-braced dampers, and automatic >0.15g seismic LPG shutoff valve.
  • Monsoon Flood Safety: Raised 1.04 m atrium plinth, deployable hydraulic aluminum Flood Barriers at street (1.12 m) & garage (0.92 m) portals, twin submersible dewatering sump pumps & brass non-return flap.
  • Thunderstorm & Lightning (IEC-62305): Discreet rear brushed-stainless ESE lightning mast (keeping the flat-topped flush-skylight spiral crown clean), roof copper Faraday ring, Type-1+2 SPD & 3 deep copper Earth Pits.

    Design specification

    Project overview

    Design intentions, climate considerations and engineering assumptions for the Dream Home concept.

    Architectural language

    Sculptural rounded white cantilevered façades, warm champagne-gold LED cove trims, panoramic glazing, full-height spiral staircase atrium and lush planted terraces.

    Climate response

    Deep overhanging curved balconies provide passive solar shading and monsoon rain protection suited to Chitwan's warm subtropical climate, supported by cross-ventilation.

    Safety & compliance

    Earthquake-resistant RCC structure (NBC Nepal), safe egress, tempered laminated atrium glazing, multi-stage water hygiene and ventilated exterior LPG separation require licensed sign-off.
